## Contests

### ICPC

The **ICPC** (International Collegiate Programming Contest) is run by the ICPC Foundation and is the most influential programming contest for university students. Because the ACM used to sponsor the contest, many people still call it the ACM contest.

The ICPC has two main stages: the Regionals and the World Finals.

Official website: <https://icpc.global>

### CCPC

Official website: <https://ccpc.io>

The China Collegiate Programming Contest.

A notable difference from the ICPC is that many universities do not reimburse participation costs for it.

## Contest format

Usually a team of three people shares one computer and may submit multiple times during the contest. Submissions are judged in real time and the result is returned immediately. Each wrong submission adds a 20-minute penalty, so the more wrong attempts, the more penalty time. A problem only counts as solved when all test cases pass. Teams are ranked by the number of problems solved; ties are broken by total time. The total time is the sum of the times for each solved problem, and the time for a problem is the number of minutes from the start of the contest until it was solved, plus that problem's penalty time.

In some ICPC-related contests, the scoreboard is frozen one hour before the end, and submissions and rankings after the freeze are hidden from other contestants.

In ICPC-related contests, contestants are allowed to bring a certain amount of printed material.

Besides the ICPC and CCPC, many other contests also use this format, such as LeetCode weekly contests and its national programming contest, and Nowcoder's beginner, practice and challenge contests.

## Season schedule

-   ICPC/CCPC online preliminaries (late August to early September)
-   ICPC/CCPC Regionals (late September to late November)
-   ICPC EC Final/CCPC Final (mid-December)
-   ICPC World Finals (April to June of the following year)

## Training guide

### Multi-University Training

Training contests held on [HDU OJ](http://acm.hdu.edu.cn) during the summer. They offer prize money and high-quality problems, and after many years they have built up a large collection of resources.

Keyword to search for on the OJ: `Multi-University Training Contest`.

### Chinese regional contests

You can find curated problem sets on [Virtual Judge](https://vjudge.net/).
